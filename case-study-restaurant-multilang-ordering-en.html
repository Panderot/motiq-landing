Case Study · Restaurant

From social media presence to professional digital platform.

A digital transformation project for a döner and pizza restaurant in the Czech Republic — consolidating fragmented ordering channels and providing professional service in three languages through a centralized web platform.

Challenge

Strong on social, missing from the web.

The restaurant had a vibrant social media presence, but no professional website. Customers couldn't view menus or place orders from a central location. Orders coming through Foodora, phone calls, and WhatsApp were becoming difficult to manage.

Additionally, customers requested service in multiple languages — a capability the existing setup couldn't deliver.

Approach

Building a centralized digital hub.

We built a comprehensive, multi-language website for the restaurant. The core objective: allow the owner to update the menu in one place, with those changes instantly reflected across all languages and all ordering channels.

We placed menu management simplicity at the center of the customer experience — the restaurant owner could manage their menu and multiple language options without writing a single line of code.

Ordering channels were clearly separated. Corporate orders via Foodora, in-person orders, phone orders, and WhatsApp inquiries — each had its own domain and clear workflow.

Result

Single source of truth, streamlined management, growing customer base.

Once the sites went live, menu changes rippled through all language versions and order systems within minutes. Customers no longer had to check multiple platforms to see what was available.

Brand identity — logo, colors, typography — became consistent across all digital touchpoints. The restaurant's professional image started on social media and now carried through to the web.
