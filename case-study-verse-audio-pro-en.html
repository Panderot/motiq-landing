Case Study · VERSE Audio Pro

Turning raw product footage into a cinematic scroll experience.

VERSE Audio Pro is a web design and development project where we turned studio and engineering footage for a premium wireless headphone launch into an Apple-style product site controlled entirely by the visitor's scroll.

Challenge

Turning raw footage into a story.

We started with studio-shot, engineering-grade exploded-view footage. It showed the product's components accurately, but on its own it wasn't a launch experience.

Our goal was to translate this raw footage into an immersive, high-conversion launch page — one that felt like the product was assembling itself as the visitor scrolled, and that matched the standard of a luxury audio brand.

Approach

We treated scroll as the timeline.

The backbone of the page is a frame-by-frame canvas sequence. However far the visitor scrolls, the product animation advances by exactly that much — playback is remapped entirely onto scroll position.

dB → Frequency → Hours

We used this "measurement rail" motif as both a progress indicator and a storytelling device — cycling through decibels, frequency range, and battery hours as the page unfolds, turning the product's technical identity into a visual rhythm.

The brushed-titanium and pure-black visual identity stayed consistent throughout every section — the material feel mattered as much as the product itself.

Result

A self-contained experience running at 60fps.

The finished site is a dependency-light scroll experience that runs smoothly at 60fps on desktop, with a separate, mobile-optimized frame set for smaller screens.

Accessibility was built in from the start — full support for the reduced-motion preference means visitors who enable it get a calmer, static experience instead of the animation.

Tech

A lean stack, carefully executed.

Rather than reach for a heavier framework, we leaned on what the browser already does well.
