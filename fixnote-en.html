Frequently asked questions

How do I install it?

Download the trial and install it on your computer; it takes a few minutes. Every feature is open for 3 days, no credit card needed.

How is the PDF created?

The "Save as PDF" button on the report screen sends the report, laid out on A4, to your phone's print screen. Choose "Save as PDF" there; it works the same way on Android and iPhone.

How do I send the PDF on WhatsApp?

The WhatsApp button opens a chat with the customer's number and a ready-made summary: report number, work carried out, parts replaced, total including VAT and the technician. You attach the PDF you saved to the same chat and send it. Enter the number with its country code, for example +44.

What kind of work is it for?

Any service that does a job at the customer's home or business and hands it over: boilers and air conditioning, household appliances, pest control, electrical and plumbing work, lift maintenance, CCTV and alarm installation. For jobs without a ready-made template, you add your own checklist.

Can I add my own checklist?

Yes. In Settings you enter the template name, the appliance type and one step per line. The template appears as a service type on the new form; its steps come ticked and you switch off any you skipped.

Is this an invoice?

No. Fixnote is a job completion report: what was done, which part was replaced, whether the customer signed it off. Keep invoicing the way you do today.

Where are my records stored?

Your reports, photos and signatures are stored in your account in a secure cloud database. They travel over an encrypted connection and stay encrypted on the server. Only people who sign in to your account can see them. Every phone that signs in to the same account sees the same records; if a phone is replaced or lost, your records are not. You can also download a single-file backup from Settings.
